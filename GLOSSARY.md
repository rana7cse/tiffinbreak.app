# TiffinBreak

A Bangla, mobile-first place where people write and read short memories from their school and college days.

## Language

**Story**:
A short personal memory written by a Writer, about one School, optionally with a cover photo.
_Avoid_: Post, article, memory

**Writer**:
A signed-in person who writes Stories and gives Love. Has a public profile.
_Avoid_: User (in UI copy), author, account

**School**:
A school or college that a Story is about. A Writer's schools are derived from the Stories they wrote, not declared on the profile.
_Avoid_: Institution, college (as a separate concept)

**Love**:
A reader's appreciation of a Story, given by tapping; one reader can give a Story up to 10 Love.
_Avoid_: Like, reaction, heart

**Feed**:
All published Stories, newest first.
_Avoid_: Timeline, home

**Report**:
A reader's flag that a Story should be taken down. A Story with enough Reports is hidden until an Admin reviews it.
_Avoid_: Flag, complaint

**Admin**:
A Writer with the right to review Reports and merge duplicate Schools.
_Avoid_: Moderator, superuser

**Merge** (of Schools):
Folding a duplicate School into another, so all its Stories now belong to the surviving School.
